# Polished Crystal: German Translation

The German version of Polished Crystal, made in a fork that tracks the English Upstream and played as a Virtual Console title on a New 3DS.

## Sources

**Upstream**:
The English Polished Crystal (Rangi42/polishedcrystal), which this fork tracks and translates.
_Avoid_: original, base game

**Kristall**:
Pokémon Kristall-Edition, the official German release of Pokémon Crystal. Its text is the German for every line Upstream kept from Crystal.
_Avoid_: German Crystal, the original

**Translation**:
The German version this project produces, in both ROMs (Regular and Faithful).
_Avoid_: localization, port

**Own translation**:
German written for this project where neither Kristall nor a later official German game has the text; always flagged for review.
_Avoid_: free translation, adaptation

## Letters

**Umlauts**:
Ä ä Ö ö Ü ü. Every font of the Translation draws them, and the naming keyboards offer them.

**ß**:
Written as Kristall writes it (mostly the new spelling: Straße, but dass; a few older forms such as erfaßt), and in Own translation as the new spelling has it. The naming keyboards have no ß key, as in Kristall.

## Pokémon

**Pokémon names**:
Kristall's names for the 251 Pokémon of Gen 2, in mixed case (BISASAM → Bisasam), and today's official German names for Polished's later ones (Pantifrost, Dummimisel). An Egg is an Ei, a Bad Egg a Schl. Ei (Emerald's form).
_Avoid_: Kristall's capitals in tables

**Drache**:
The Dragon type and the Dragon egg group, as the games write it since Gen 4.
_Avoid_: Drachen (Kristall's name for the type)

**Abilities**:
The official German names from before Gen 9, the era of Polished's mechanics: Tempomacher (Own Tempo), Fußangel (Tangled Feet), Aufheber (Poison Heal), Hagelalarm (Snow Warning, which summons hail in Polished).
_Avoid_: Gleichmut, Taumelschritt, Giftheilung, Schneeschauer

**Egg groups**:
The official German names (Monster, Feld, Humanotyp, Amorph, Unbekannt …); in Faithful also Wasser 1, Wasser 2, Wasser 3. Regular's own names for the water groups are an Own translation: Amphibie (Water 1), Fisch (Water 2), Wirbellos (Water 3).
_Avoid_: Wirbellose

**Type chart labels**:
The Type Chart screen names the attacker ANGR and the defender VERT (Kristall's abbreviations for Angriff and Verteidigung) and heads its columns NRML, KAMPF, FLUG, GIFT, BODEN, GEST, KÄFR, GEIS, STHL, FEUR, WASR, PFL, ELEK, PSYC, EIS, DRCH, UNLI, FEE.

## Moves

**Move names**:
Kristall's names for the moves of Gen 2, in mixed case (DONNERBLITZ → Donnerblitz), and today's official German names for Polished's later ones. Where a Kristall name was renamed later, the choice per move is: Konfusstrahl, Staffette, Giftblick, Schnabel, Wandler, Gesichte.
_Avoid_: Konfustrahl, Stafette, Schlangenblick, Pikser, Verwandler, Scharfblick

**Wertewechsel**:
Skill Swap, by its official name from Gen 3 to 7; today's name doesn't fit the 12-character limit.
_Avoid_: Fähigkeitstausch

**Säuselstimme**:
Disarming Voice (in Polished's English squeezed to "Disarm Voice").

**Frischsnack**, **Furienhieb**, **Heillicht**:
Own translations of Polished's own moves: Fresh Snack (replaces Milchgetränk and Weichei), Fury Strikes (replaces Furienschlag and Kratzfurie), Healing Light (replaces Mondschein, Morgengrauen and Synthese).
_Avoid_: Furienhiebe, Lichtheilung

**VM**:
An HM is a VM (Versteckte Maschine), as in Kristall: VM01. A TM stays a TM.
_Avoid_: HM

## Items

**Item names**:
Kristall's names for the items of Gen 2, in mixed case, and today's official German names for Polished's later ones. Where a Kristall name was renamed later and both fit, the choice per item is: Upgrade, Flegmon-Rute, Fokusband, Krummlöffel (today's), Rote Haut (Kristall's). Where today's name is over 12 characters, Kristall's stays: X-Tempo, X-Treffer, Angriffplus, Hackattack, Schattenglas, Schutzband, Drachenhaut, Türöffner.
_Avoid_: Up-Grade, Flegmonrute, Fokus-Band, Krümmlöffel, Rote Schuppe, X-Initiative, X-Genauigkeit, X-Volltreffer, Spitzer Schnabel, Schattenbrille, Schutzsticker, Drachenschuppe, Schlüsselkarte

**Pokéball**:
One word, as today's games write it (Kristall: #BALL); likewise Poképuppe.

**Older official item names**:
Seegesang (Shell Bell), Schwerschwf. (Lagging Tail) and Liftöffner (Lift Key), the official names of earlier games, because today's don't fit 12 characters.
_Avoid_: Muschelglocke, Schwerschweif, Aufzugsschlüssel

**Wutkeks**, **Honig**, **Megablock**:
One name per item in both ROMs, for the items Regular renames (Cake of Rage, Sweet Honey, Guard Stats) and Faithful doesn't (RageCandyBar, Honey, Guard Spec.).
_Avoid_: Wutkuchen, Süßer Honig, Werteschutz

**Item abbreviations**:
Official names over 12 characters, each with one word shortened: Marmoria-Kr., Fäh.-Kapsel, Fäh.Pflaster, Schwä.schutz, Offens.weste, Schutzpolst., Plat.schuhe, Fehl.schutz, Bizarroserv., Neutralschm., Gez. Würfel. Fäh. always stands for Fähigkeiten.

**Link-Kabel**, **Kronkorken**:
Linking Cord and Bottle Cap, in place of the shortened official names (Verbindungsschnur, Silberkronkorken).
_Avoid_: Verb.schnur, Silberkronk.

**Own item names**:
Rüstung (Armor Suit), Kuriosum (Odd Souvenir), Minzblatt (Mint Leaf), Mulch, Federbox (Wing Case), Typentabelle (Type Chart), GBC-Player (GBC Sounds, after HeartGold's GB-Player), Orangeticket, and the Lures Herbparfüm, Kraftparfüm, Unheilparfüm (after Let's Go's Lockparfüm).

**Pockets**:
Item-Tasche, Medizin-Tasche, Ball-Tasche, TM-Tasche, Beeren-Tasche, Basis-Items (Kristall's, with Medizin and Beeren from HeartGold). The Bag's tabs read Items, Med., Bälle, TM/VM, Beeren, Bas.; the label under the Bag reads Items, Medizin, Bälle, TM/VM, Beeren, Basis.
_Avoid_: Taschen named after the English (Key-Tasche, Medi-Tasche)

**Zurück**:
Leaving a list, as Kristall writes it in the Bag and Marts; Kristall's trade screen says Abbrechen instead.
_Avoid_: Abbrechen in lists

## Trainers

**Trainer classes**:
Kristall's classes in mixed case (TEENAGER → Teenager), and today's official German class where Upstream's English uses today's: Vogel-Profi (Bird Keeper), Schüler (Schoolboy), Seher (Psychic), Rowdy (Biker), Butler (Darach). Polished's own classes: Cosplayer, Paar (Couple), Duo, Vorstand (Rocket Executive), Ex-Top 4 (Lorelei, Agatha), Steinsammler (Steven); Züchterin and Sammler drop the official "Pokémon-". The stranger in Elm's lab is Fremder Junge, the shrine maiden Schreinmaid Rei.
_Avoid_: Vogelfänger, Schuljunge, Psycho, Biker, Kammerdiener

**Feminine classes**:
Women get Leiterin, Trainerin and Wächterin; Blanche and Jacky stay Leiter and Trainer. Ass-Trainer and Skifahrer stay as Kristall writes them for women too.

**Pairs**:
Twins, couples and duos write their two names without spaces (Nora&Jo, Erik&Sara), and so does the class Sen.&Jun. (Sr. and Jr.). Jessie & James are the class Jessie with the name "& James".
_Avoid_: Nora & Jo, Sen. & Jun.

**Trainer names**:
Kristall's name for every trainer from Crystal, in mixed case, also where Polished renamed the trainer (Polished's Wade is Kristall's Greg). Trainers from other games take that game's official German name (FireRed/LeafGreen, HeartGold/SoulSilver); characters take their official German name (Atlas, Athena, Lance and Lambda for the Rocket Executives). Polished's own trainers keep Polished's name, unless an official German one exists. An official name over the limit takes its short form (Josie, Stanis, Tina, Hein, Lee, Ritsu). Names repeat across trainers, as in Kristall.

**Estefan**:
Psychic Inver, by his official German name.
_Avoid_: Inver

**Battle Tower names**:
The Battle Tower's trainers keep their reference names, except the official characters (Viktor for Palmer, Distmar for Thorton) and the references that only work under another name: Tajiri for Satoshi, Fabre for Tajiri, Bates for Darwin, Venus for Valentine.

**Wonder Trade names**:
The original trainers of Wonder Trade Pokémon: the official German name of each Pokémon character or version where it has at most 7 letters (Krista, Lance, Wilma); every other name stays as Upstream writes it (Alice, Butch, Cassidy).

**In-game trades**:
Crystal's trade partners and their Pokémon keep Kristall's names (Ralf with Herakles, Tobi, Carmen with Dori, Paul). The Heracross from Pewter City is Herkules: Kristall calls its trainer Paul, so it can't keep Upstream's nickname Paul. The other nicknames stay as Upstream writes them (Mimic, Joey, Chance, Gail, Clarence, Batty).

**Nicknames**:
Trainers' Pokémon nicknames are German where the English is a word play (Hitzkopf, Kanonade, Wurzelwut) and stay where they're a name, a reference or a foreign word (Milky, Bruce, Jackie, Antorcha). Yellow's team takes the German manga names (Amoni, Ratzi, Raups, Dodi, Geon).

**Default names**:
The player is Chris, Krista, Crys or Krys by picture, the rival Silber, the Magikarp record holder Angelo (Kristall's names).
_Avoid_: Kris, Silver, Ralph
