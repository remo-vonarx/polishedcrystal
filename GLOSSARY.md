# Polished Crystal: German Translation

The German version of Polished Crystal, made in a fork that tracks the English Upstream and played as a Virtual Console title on a New 3DS.

## Sources

**Upstream**:
The English Polished Crystal (Rangi42/polishedcrystal), which this fork tracks and translates.
_Avoid_: original, base game

**Kristall**:
Pokémon Kristall-Edition, the official German release of Pokémon Crystal. Its text is the German for every line Upstream kept from Crystal.
_Avoid_: German Crystal, the original

**Translation**:
The German version this project produces, in both ROMs (Regular and Faithful).
_Avoid_: localization, port

**Own translation**:
German written for this project where neither Kristall nor a later official German game has the text; always flagged for review.
_Avoid_: free translation, adaptation

## Letters

**Umlauts**:
Ä ä Ö ö Ü ü. Every font of the Translation draws them, and the naming keyboards offer them.

**ß**:
Written as Kristall writes it (mostly the new spelling: Straße, but dass; a few older forms such as erfaßt), and in Own translation as the new spelling has it. The naming keyboards have no ß key, as in Kristall.

## Pokémon

**Pokémon names**:
Kristall's names for the 251 Pokémon of Gen 2, in mixed case (BISASAM → Bisasam), and today's official German names for Polished's later ones (Pantifrost, Dummimisel). An Egg is an Ei, a Bad Egg a Schl. Ei (Emerald's form).
_Avoid_: Kristall's capitals in tables

**Drache**:
The Dragon type and the Dragon egg group, as the games write it since Gen 4.
_Avoid_: Drachen (Kristall's name for the type)

**Abilities**:
The official German names from before Gen 9, the era of Polished's mechanics: Tempomacher (Own Tempo), Fußangel (Tangled Feet), Aufheber (Poison Heal), Hagelalarm (Snow Warning, which summons hail in Polished).
_Avoid_: Gleichmut, Taumelschritt, Giftheilung, Schneeschauer

**Egg groups**:
The official German names (Monster, Feld, Humanotyp, Amorph, Unbekannt …); in Faithful also Wasser 1, Wasser 2, Wasser 3. Regular's own names for the water groups are an Own translation: Amphibie (Water 1), Fisch (Water 2), Wirbellos (Water 3).
_Avoid_: Wirbellose

**Type chart labels**:
The Type Chart screen names the attacker ANGR and the defender VERT (Kristall's abbreviations for Angriff and Verteidigung) and heads its columns NRML, KAMPF, FLUG, GIFT, BODEN, GEST, KÄFR, GEIS, STHL, FEUR, WASR, PFL, ELEK, PSYC, EIS, DRCH, UNLI, FEE.

## Moves

**Move names**:
Kristall's names for the moves of Gen 2, in mixed case (DONNERBLITZ → Donnerblitz), and today's official German names for Polished's later ones. Where a Kristall name was renamed later, the choice per move is: Konfusstrahl, Staffette, Giftblick, Schnabel, Wandler, Gesichte.
_Avoid_: Konfustrahl, Stafette, Schlangenblick, Pikser, Verwandler, Scharfblick

**Wertewechsel**:
Skill Swap, by its official name from Gen 3 to 7; today's name doesn't fit the 12-character limit.
_Avoid_: Fähigkeitstausch

**Säuselstimme**:
Disarming Voice (in Polished's English squeezed to "Disarm Voice").

**Frischsnack**, **Furienhieb**, **Heillicht**:
Own translations of Polished's own moves: Fresh Snack (replaces Milchgetränk and Weichei), Fury Strikes (replaces Furienschlag and Kratzfurie), Healing Light (replaces Mondschein, Morgengrauen and Synthese).
_Avoid_: Furienhiebe, Lichtheilung

**VM**:
An HM is a VM (Versteckte Maschine), as in Kristall: VM01. A TM stays a TM.
_Avoid_: HM

## Items

**Item names**:
Kristall's names for the items of Gen 2, in mixed case, and today's official German names for Polished's later ones. Where a Kristall name was renamed later and both fit, the choice per item is: Upgrade, Flegmon-Rute, Fokusband, Krummlöffel (today's), Rote Haut (Kristall's). Where today's name is over 12 characters, Kristall's stays: X-Tempo, X-Treffer, Angriffplus, Hackattack, Schattenglas, Schutzband, Drachenhaut, Türöffner.
_Avoid_: Up-Grade, Flegmonrute, Fokus-Band, Krümmlöffel, Rote Schuppe, X-Initiative, X-Genauigkeit, X-Volltreffer, Spitzer Schnabel, Schattenbrille, Schutzsticker, Drachenschuppe, Schlüsselkarte

**Pokéball**:
One word, as today's games write it (Kristall: #BALL); likewise Poképuppe.

**Older official item names**:
Seegesang (Shell Bell), Schwerschwf. (Lagging Tail) and Liftöffner (Lift Key), the official names of earlier games, because today's don't fit 12 characters.
_Avoid_: Muschelglocke, Schwerschweif, Aufzugsschlüssel

**Wutkeks**, **Honig**, **Megablock**:
One name per item in both ROMs, for the items Regular renames (Cake of Rage, Sweet Honey, Guard Stats) and Faithful doesn't (RageCandyBar, Honey, Guard Spec.).
_Avoid_: Wutkuchen, Süßer Honig, Werteschutz

**Item abbreviations**:
Official names over 12 characters, each with one word shortened: Marmoria-Kr., Fäh.-Kapsel, Fäh.Pflaster, Schwä.schutz, Offens.weste, Schutzpolst., Plat.schuhe, Fehl.schutz, Bizarroserv., Neutralschm., Gez. Würfel. Fäh. always stands for Fähigkeiten.

**Link-Kabel**, **Kronkorken**:
Linking Cord and Bottle Cap, in place of the shortened official names (Verbindungsschnur, Silberkronkorken).
_Avoid_: Verb.schnur, Silberkronk.

**Own item names**:
Rüstung (Armor Suit), Kuriosum (Odd Souvenir), Minzblatt (Mint Leaf), Mulch, Federbox (Wing Case), Typentabelle (Type Chart), GBC-Player (GBC Sounds, after HeartGold's GB-Player), Orangeticket, and the Lures Herbparfüm, Kraftparfüm, Unheilparfüm (after Let's Go's Lockparfüm).

**Pockets**:
Item-Tasche, Medizin-Tasche, Ball-Tasche, TM-Tasche, Beeren-Tasche, Basis-Items (Kristall's, with Medizin and Beeren from HeartGold). The Bag's tabs read Items, Med., Bälle, TM/VM, Beeren, Bas.; the label under the Bag reads Items, Medizin, Bälle, TM/VM, Beeren, Basis.
_Avoid_: Taschen named after the English (Key-Tasche, Medi-Tasche)

**Zurück**:
Leaving a list, as Kristall writes it in the Bag and Marts; Kristall's trade screen says Abbrechen instead.
_Avoid_: Abbrechen in lists

## Trainers

**Trainer classes**:
Kristall's classes in mixed case (TEENAGER → Teenager), and today's official German class where Upstream's English uses today's: Vogel-Profi (Bird Keeper), Schüler (Schoolboy), Seher (Psychic), Rowdy (Biker), Butler (Darach). Polished's own classes: Cosplayer, Paar (Couple), Duo, Vorstand (Rocket Executive), Ex-Top 4 (Lorelei, Agatha), Steinsammler (Steven); Züchterin and Sammler drop the official "Pokémon-". The stranger in Elm's lab is Fremder Junge, the shrine maiden Schreinmaid Rei.
_Avoid_: Vogelfänger, Schuljunge, Psycho, Biker, Kammerdiener

**Feminine classes**:
Women get Leiterin, Trainerin and Wächterin; Blanche and Jacky stay Leiter and Trainer. Ass-Trainer and Skifahrer stay as Kristall writes them for women too.

**Pairs**:
Twins, couples and duos write their two names without spaces (Nora&Jo, Erik&Sara), and so does the class Sen.&Jun. (Sr. and Jr.). Jessie & James are the class Jessie with the name "& James".
_Avoid_: Nora & Jo, Sen. & Jun.

**Trainer names**:
Kristall's name for every trainer from Crystal, in mixed case, also where Polished renamed the trainer (Polished's Wade is Kristall's Greg). Trainers from other games take that game's official German name (FireRed/LeafGreen, HeartGold/SoulSilver); characters take their official German name (Atlas, Athena, Lance and Lambda for the Rocket Executives). Polished's own trainers keep Polished's name, unless an official German one exists. An official name over the limit takes its short form (Josie, Stanis, Tina, Hein, Lee, Ritsu). Names repeat across trainers, as in Kristall.

**Estefan**:
Psychic Inver, by his official German name.
_Avoid_: Inver

**Battle Tower names**:
The Battle Tower's trainers keep their reference names, except the official characters (Viktor for Palmer, Distmar for Thorton) and the references that only work under another name: Tajiri for Satoshi, Fabre for Tajiri, Bates for Darwin, Venus for Valentine.

**Wonder Trade names**:
The original trainers of Wonder Trade Pokémon: the official German name of each Pokémon character or version where it has at most 7 letters (Krista, Lance, Wilma); every other name stays as Upstream writes it (Alice, Butch, Cassidy).

**In-game trades**:
Crystal's trade partners and their Pokémon keep Kristall's names (Ralf with Herakles, Tobi, Carmen with Dori, Paul). The Heracross from Pewter City is Herkules: Kristall calls its trainer Paul, so it can't keep Upstream's nickname Paul. The other nicknames stay as Upstream writes them (Mimic, Joey, Chance, Gail, Clarence, Batty).

**Nicknames**:
Trainers' Pokémon nicknames are German where the English is a word play (Hitzkopf, Kanonade, Wurzelwut) and stay where they're a name, a reference or a foreign word (Milky, Bruce, Jackie, Antorcha). Yellow's team takes the German manga names (Amoni, Ratzi, Raups, Dodi, Geon).

**Default names**:
The player is Chris, Krista, Crys or Krys by picture, the rival Silber, the Magikarp record holder Angelo (Kristall's names).
_Avoid_: Kris, Silver, Ralph

## Places

**Place names**:
Kristall's names for Crystal's places, in mixed case (NEUBORKIA → Neuborkia, SEE DES ZORNS → See des Zorns), with the ß back where Kristall's capitals write SS (Siegesstraße). Where Upstream uses today's English name, today's German one: Glockenturm (Bell Tower), Unterführung (Underground Path). Where Upstream keeps Crystal's, Kristall's: Digdas Höhle, Indigo Plateau, Ruhestätte (House of Souls, Kristall's sign). Chosen otherwise: Pokémon-Villa (today's name; Kristall and FireRed: Pokémon-Haus), M.S. Aqua (Fast Ship, named after the ship as in HeartGold/SoulSilver), Radioturm Lavandia (Kristall: Radioturm Lav.).
_Avoid_: Zinnturm, Untergrund, Pokémon-Haus, Schnellboot as the place, Digda-Höhle, Indigo-Plateau

**Bronzeturm**:
The Burned Tower's old name in both ROMs, as Kristall has it (Faithful: Brass Tower, Regular: Gong Tower).
_Avoid_: Gongturm

**Places from later games**:
Their official German names: Glockenklangpfad, Felsklippentor, Felsschlundhöhle, Sinjoh-Ruinen and Trysteriumaltar (HeartGold/SoulSilver); Pokémon-Labor, Nabelfels and Ferneiland (FireRed/LeafGreen, Emerald); the Pokéwalker's routes Stürmische Küste, Holpriger Pfad, Gruselhöhle, Finstere Höhle, Malerische Küste, Lauschiger Wald, Sonniger Strand, Stille Höhle and Goldgelber Wald; the anime's Shamouti, Insel des Feuers, Insel des Eises, Insel der Blitze and Valencia Insel.

**Own place names**:
Own translations for Polished's places: Viola-Stadtrand, Rosalia-Bucht, Dukatia-Hafen, Magnettunnel, Route 32: Küste (and 34, 35), Trüber Sumpf, Schneegipfel, Felsiger Strand, Uraga-Kanal, Schreinruinen, Shamouti-Tunnel, Shamouti-Küste, Prismania-Uni, Zinnober-Vulkan, Kap von Azuria, Glücksinsel, Pokémon-Liga: Tor (Fly writes Pokémon-Liga).
_Avoid_: Küste Route 32, Universität Prismania

**Pokégear line breaks**:
A place name over 11 letters takes two lines on the Pokégear and the Town Map, broken where Kristall breaks it, with a hyphen inside a word (Einheits-/tunnel, Sieges-/straße). Two break elsewhere: Stein-/eichenwald (Kristall: Steinei-/chenwald) and Felsschlund/höhle, without a hyphen, since "Felsschlund-" doesn't fit.

**Ships**:
The M.S. Aqua and the M.S. Anne, as Kristall and the German Red/Blue name them.
_Avoid_: S.S. Aqua, S.S. Anne

**Orden**:
A badge, by Kristall's names: Flügelorden, Insektorden, Basisorden, Phantomorden, Stahlorden, Faustorden, Eisorden, Drachenorden; Felsorden, Quellorden, Donnerorden, Farborden, Sumpforden, Seelenorden, Vulkanorden, Erdorden. Receiving one: "<PLAYER> erhält den Flügelorden."
_Avoid_: Badge, Plakette

**Wo?**:
Fly's question on the Town Map, as Kristall writes it.

**Kampffabrik**, **Event-Pokémon**:
The place of capture in the summary for a Battle Factory rental and for an event Pokémon.

## Inherited text

**Inherited text**:
A text whose English is Crystal's word for word. It has Kristall's German, with its words and line breaks; only names, terms and the case of capitals follow the Translation.
_Avoid_: copied text

**Kept capitals**:
Kristall's capitals stay where Upstream's English shouts or stresses in capitals too (NICHT, HILFE, BIEP, the roaring man on Route 32), and in abbreviations (KP, AP, PC, TM, VM, DJ, TV, EG, ID, IQ, SZ, PKMN, ANGR, VERT, INIT).

**PokéCom**:
The Pokégear, as Kristall calls it (#COM).
_Avoid_: Pokégear, Pokékom

**PokéKom-Center**:
The Pokémon Communication Center in Dukatia City, Kristall's #KOM-CENTER.
_Avoid_: PokéCom-Center

**PKMN-Arena**:
A Gym in signs and dialogue where Kristall writes PKMN-ARENA; Arena alone where it does.
_Avoid_: Pokémon-Arena

**Schnellboot**:
The Fast Ship as a common noun in dialogue (das Schnellboot, Schnellboot-Pier), as Kristall has it. The ship's name is M.S. Aqua.

**nachfliegen**, **zurückfliegen**:
Fly's verbs in dialogue, lowercase, where Kristall writes nachFLIEGEN.

**Vogel-Profis**:
The plural of the class Vogel-Profi in dialogue.
_Avoid_: Vogelfänger

**Mr. Psycho**:
The psychic in Saffronia City, by Kristall's name on the sign of their house; the trainer class is Seher.

**Flegmon-Ruten**, **Para-Heiler**:
Items in dialogue follow today's spelling of their names.
_Avoid_: Flegmonruten, Paraheiler

**Semicolon**:
The font has none: a semicolon in Kristall's text is a comma.

## Receiving and shared texts

**Several items**:
A number with an x and the name as it is, where there is more than one ("3x Trank", "99x Beleber"); one has no x ("1 Trank"). The verb stays as Kristall has it (kostet, wurde).
_Avoid_: 3 x Trank, 3 Tränke

**Several wings**:
Wings from a headbutted tree always have the x, for one too ("1x Heilfeder").
_Avoid_: Heilfedern

**GP**:
Battle Points (Gewinnpunkte), always the abbreviation after a number (25 GP).
_Avoid_: BP

**Tauschecke**:
The counter that trades items for GP.

**Richter-Maschine**:
The Judge Machine; what it does is bewerten (to judge the Pokémon's overall strength).
_Avoid_: Bewertungsmaschine

**Super-Spezialtraining**:
Hyper Training.
_Avoid_: Spezialtraining

**Trainerpass**:
The Trainer Card, where Kristall has TRAINERPASS.

**übertragen**:
A Pokémon or Egg with no room in the party "wurde auf {Box} übertragen".
_Avoid_: geschickt, gesendet

**Weiter**:
The menu entry the clock's question names (Continue), as Kristall's main menu has it.

**Karten-Modul**, **Radio-Modul**, **Zusatzset**:
The Map Card, the Radio Card and the Expansion Card.

**Decorations**:
Kristall's decoration names in mixed case: Baumpflanze, Tropenpflanze, Riesenpflanze, Goldtrophäe, Silbertrophäe, Surfer Pikachu-P.; a Pokémon's poster and doll have a hyphen (Marill-Poster, Bisasam-Puppe), a big one is Riesiges Relaxo. Super NES, Nintendo 64, GameCube and Wii stay.

## Battle

**Stat names**:
In full in battle messages: Angriff, Verteidigung, Initiative, Spez.-Angriff, Spez.-Verteidigung, Genauigkeit, Fluchtwert; Statuswert where the English says "stats" (Curse).
_Avoid_: ANGR, VER, INIT (Kristall's abbreviations in these messages), Spezial-Verteidigung

**Possessive 's in battle**:
Every Pokémon's and the player's name in a battle text takes 's ("Endivie's Angriff steigt!", "Arktos's Hagelalarm"). Outside battle, and on the link record, names keep Kristall's plain s (Linds, Bills, "<PLAYER>s Statistik").
_Avoid_: Endivies

**Stat message**:
Name and stat, then the box scrolls by itself to the verb, as Kristall's strong stat changes do: steigt, sinkt; stark (two stages); drastisch (three); "steigt durch {Item}" for an item.

**ben.**:
benutzt, before the move's name, as Kristall shortens it ("Endivie / ben. Solarstrahl!").

**Gegn.**:
The foe's Pokémon in battle texts (Gegn. Natu), Kristall's abbreviation.
_Avoid_: Feind

**Dein**, **Gegner**:
Whose side an effect ends on ("Dein / Bodyguard / schwindet!"), as Kristall has it.

**Kampf**, **Beutel**, **Flucht**:
The battle menu; Kristall shortens the first to KMPF. The Safari Game has Köder and Stein, the Bug-Catching Contest and the Safari Game count Balls as Ball×.
_Avoid_: KMPF, Angriff, Tasche

**Tausch**, **Status**, **Zurück**:
The menu of a Pokémon in battle (Switch, Summary, Cancel), Kristall's words.

**GIF**, **PAR**, **SLF**, **BRT**, **GFR**:
The status labels in battle, as Kristall has them.

**KP**, **EP**, **AP**:
HP, Exp. Points and PP, on the bars and in the texts ("157 EP!"). The power of a move is P in the move box.

**Geist**:
The unidentified Ghost's name in battle, and who speaks ("Geist: / Verschwinde…").

**Pairs of trainers**:
Twins, Sen.&Jun., couples, duos and Jessie & James take the plural ("möchten kämpfen!", "setzen", "wurden besiegt!").

**Statistik**, **Gew.**, **Verl.**, **Patt**:
The link battle record, Kristall's words; the result screen says Gewonnen, Verloren, Unentschieden.

**Turm-Tycoon Viktor**, **Fabrikchef Distmar**:
Palmer and Thorton; their texts follow HeartGold/SoulSilver's German.

**Last-Pokémon lines**:
A trainer's line at their last Pokémon follows the German of the game the English comes from (mostly HeartGold/SoulSilver).

## Menus

**Status**, **Tausch**, **Zurück**:
Summary, Switch and Cancel in every menu of a Pokémon, as Kristall has them. Zurück also closes lists and the START menu (Cancel, Exit, Quit); Abbrechen only in the link trade.
_Avoid_: Bericht, Wechseln (in menus of a Pokémon)

**Ja**, **Nein**:
Yes and No. Every box that holds them is as wide as Kristall's.

**Angr**, **Ver**, **Init**, **Spez.Ang**, **Spez.Ver**:
The stats in tables (summary, level up, hyper training), Kristall's words. The judge machine's drawn labels have Sp.Ang and Sp.Ver. Item messages name the stat in full, as in battle; the Wing Case's help line alone shortens three of them ("Erhöht Vert.", "Erhöht Spez.-Angr.", "Erhöht Spez.-Vert.").
_Avoid_: Vert., Spezial

**Fähigk.**, **Fundort**, **Attacke**, **Item**, **EP**:
The tabs drawn on the summary pages. Fähigkeit doesn't fit its tab.

**Bes.**:
Besitz, in the Mart's box that counts how many you already have.
_Avoid_: Btl.

**Gib**, **Nimm**, **Lies**, **Bewege**:
The item menus (Give, Take, Read, Move).

**Lösch**, **Ende**, **klein**, **Groß**:
The bottom row of the naming screens, as Kristall. The first word names the letters it switches to.

**Text-Tempo**, **Kampfanimation**, **Kampfstil**, **Rahmen**, **Wechsel**, **Folgend**:
Options, Kristall's words. Own: Auto-Textlauf, Schriftart, Tastatur, Spitznamen, Turbotreter (the official Running Shoes), Dreh-Tempo, Uhrzeit-Format, Pokédex-Maße, Ansage (Predict), Fertig (Done).

**Option**, **Optionen**:
The START menu's entry and the main menu's entry, as Kristall has them.

**FP**, **Wesen**, **Fähigkeit**, **Phys/Spez-Split**:
Options of a new game (EVs, Natures, Abilities, the physical/special split).
_Avoid_: EV, Basispunkte

**Charakter**:
The heading above a Pokémon's characteristic; the 30 characteristics are the official ones.

**Ablegen**, **Mitnehmen**, **Freilassen**, **Wechseln**, **Name**, **Design**:
The Boxes (Deposit, Withdraw, Release, Change, Rename, Theme). The mode button says SEL MENÜ, SEL TAUSCH, SEL ITEM.
_Avoid_: Thema

**Machs gut,**:
The Boxes' goodbye to a released Pokémon, instead of Kristall's Ade.
_Avoid_: Ade

**Idylle**, **Klassisch**:
Box designs Bliss (the Windows XP picture) and Classic. Designs named after types carry the type's name.

**Bills PC**, **Eichs PC**, **<PLAYER>s PC**, **Ausloggen**:
The PC menu, with Kristall's plain s.

**Erspartes**:
Money saved with Mom. Kristall's Ersparnisse is one letter too long for Polished's seven digits.
_Avoid_: Ersparnisse

**Fleiß**, **Potenzial**:
The judge machine's pages (Effort, Potential).

**Melden**, **Erstes**, **Zweites**, **Drittes**, **L.Gegner**:
Choosing Pokémon for the Battle Tower and Battle Factory. L.Gegner marks the beaten opponent's Pokémon in the swap list.

**GP**:
Gewinnpunkte (Battle Points); Pkt for points in the corner boxes.

**Glück**:
The trendy phrase that opens the Glücksinsel (Lucky). **Nichts** is the trend before the player sets one.

**RH-Einsteiger**, **RH-Meister**:
The Hall of Fame's headings, Kristall's words.

**24 hours, metric**:
What a new game starts with, as in Kristall; Upstream starts with 12 hours and imperial units.

## Descriptions

**ANGR-Wert**, **VERT-Wert**, **INIT-Wert**, **SPEZ. ANG**, **SPEZ. VER**:
The stats in move and item descriptions, Kristall's words and spelling (only the Offens.weste has no room for the space). Ability descriptions name the stat in full (Angriff, Verteidigung, Initiative, Spez.-Angriff) where two lines allow it; Blitzfänger has Spez.-Angr., Bruchrüstung VERT and INIT.
_Avoid_: Spez.-Angriff in a move's or item's text

**in Notfällen**:
"In a pinch", for Berries and abilities that work at low HP; the official wording of X and Y.
_Avoid_: bei Gefahr, bei wenig KP

**(Tragen)**:
"(Hold)" at the end of an item's text, as in Kristall.

**Hebt**, **Senkt**, **Füllt … auf**, **evtl.**, **enorm**:
Raises, lowers, restores, may, sharply, as in Kristall's descriptions.

**Sympathie**:
Friendship in item texts (Friend Ball, Luxury Ball, the EV Berries, Soothe Bell), Kristall's word.
_Avoid_: Freundschaft, Zuneigung

**Wertzuwachs**, **ANGR-Zuwachs**, …:
The stat gain (EVs) of the Macho Brace and the Power items.

**Körperattacke**, **Spezialattacke**, **Status-Attacke**:
Physical, special and status move, after Kristall's "Körperattacken".
_Avoid_: physische Attacke

**Entwickler**:
A Pokémon that can still evolve (Eviolite), as in Kristall's Moon Ball.

**Saug-**, **Klammer-**, **Lärm-**, **Serien-**, **Wind-Attacken**, **Barrieren**:
Draining, trapping, sound-based, multi-hit and wind moves, and the screens, after the official texts.

**eff.**:
Short for effektiv in the texts of the Expertengurt and Schwä.schutz alone, where the full word doesn't fit.

**Seesprint-Fähre**:
The Seagallop Ferry (PokéWiki).
